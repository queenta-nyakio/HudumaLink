Simple Process

How HudumaLink works

From discovering a provider to reviewing a completed service, the process is straightforward.

1

Create an Account

Register as a customer looking for services or as a provider who wants to list their professional services.

2

Find or Offer Services

Customers browse available providers and submit requests. Providers manage their listings and respond to incoming requests.

3

Complete and Review

Providers update request statuses, and customers can leave ratings and feedback after their requests are marked completed.
